## Bathroom & Kitchen Specification for Developers, Architects & Designers

For over 30 years, Zest Bathrooms & Kitchens has worked alongside developers, architects, interior designers, consultants and construction teams to deliver bathroom and kitchen solutions for residential, hospitality, leisure and commercial projects across the UK.

We specialise in the specification and supply of branded bathrooms and SieMatic kitchens, combining detailed product knowledge with technical expertise and a design-led approach.

From private residences and residential developments to hotels, leisure spaces, wellness projects and commercial schemes, we support projects of varying scales and requirements. Our work extends across the UK, with projects in Chester, Manchester, Liverpool, London, Birmingham and locations nationwide.

From individual developments to large-scale multi-unit schemes, we work collaboratively with project teams to ensure every specification meets the design intent, performance requirements and budget of the project.

## Supporting Projects From Concept to Completion

We understand that every project has its own requirements.

For developers, the focus may be on consistency, value engineering, programme and reliable supply. For architects and interior designers, maintaining the integrity of the design vision and achieving the right aesthetic may be paramount.

Luxury Residential Developments

## Specification That Adds Value

Bathrooms and kitchens play an important role in how a development is perceived.

The right products can elevate the overall design, strengthen the quality of the specification and contribute to the appeal and long-term value of a property.

Zest has extensive experience supplying bathrooms and kitchens for luxury residential developments, working with project teams to establish specifications that combine visual impact with performance, durability and commercial considerations.

From individual high-end properties to large-scale apartment developments, we provide solutions that can be consistently specified across a scheme while retaining the quality and design standards expected of premium developments.

Our project experience includes major residential schemes across the North West, including [Deansgate Square](/projects/renaker-deansgate-square), [Viadux](/projects/viadux), [Weir Mill](/projects/weir-mill) and [Crusader Mill](/projects/crusader-mill).
