About Zest Bathrooms & Kitchensr

For more than three decades, Zest Bathrooms & Kitchens has been creating beautifully considered interiors from our showroom in Chester. Established in 1992, we have grown from a specialist bathroom showroom into an independent destination for luxury bathrooms, contemporary interiors and [premium SieMatic kitchens](/kitchens/siematic-kitchens).

Our approach is rooted in a simple belief: exceptional design should feel personal. Rather than following passing trends, we bring together considered design, exceptional materials and some of the world’s leading bathroom and kitchen brands to create spaces that are made to be lived in.

Based in the heart of Chester, we work with homeowners, designers, architects and property professionals across Cheshire, the Wirral, North Wales and throughout the UK

## Our Chester Showroom

Our showroom at Brookdale Place, Chester is designed to be experienced rather than simply viewed online.

Here, you can explore bathroom displays, kitchen environments, materials, finishes, brassware and leading design brands in person. Seeing and touching products gives you a much better understanding of scale, texture, craftsmanship and quality — details that are difficult to appreciate from a screen.

Whether you are beginning to explore ideas for a new bathroom, planning a kitchen renovation or developing a larger residential project, our Chester showroom provides an opportunity to discuss your requirements with our team.

Private design consultations are available by appointment for clients who would like dedicated time to explore their project.

## A Heritage Built Around Design

Zest was founded with a passion for bathrooms and an appreciation for good design. Over the years, our knowledge, showroom and [brand portfolio](/brands) have evolved, but our independent approach has remained at the heart of the business.

Today, our Chester showroom brings together carefully selected bathroom collections, SieMatic kitchens, brassware, furniture, surfaces and specialist wellness products. Each brand is chosen for its design integrity, craftsmanship and ability to contribute something distinctive to an interior.

Our experience allows us to look beyond individual products and consider how materials, proportions, finishes and functionality work together as a complete space.

## Independent by Nature

As an independent kitchen and bathroom showroom, Zest is not tied to a single approach to interior design. Our independence allows us to curate products and brands based on quality, design and suitability, helping clients explore different possibilities for their home or project.

From refined contemporary bathrooms and spa-inspired spaces to architectural SieMatic kitchens, we believe the best interiors are those where every element has a reason for being there.

It is this considered approach that has helped Zest build long-standing relationships with clients, designers, architects, developers and leading manufacturers.

## Bringing Global Design to Cheshire

Our work in Chester is connected to an international network of respected manufacturers and design brands. We work with carefully selected names across kitchens, bathrooms, brassware, furniture and wellness, bringing distinctive European and international design to clients across Cheshire and beyond.

Our brand portfolio includes SieMatic, Gessi, AXOR, Duravit, Bette, Oasis, Novello, Cielo and other specialist manufacturers.

Rather than simply offering a catalogue of products, we believe our value lies in knowing the brands, understanding their strengths and helping clients identify the right products for their individual space.

## Designed for the Way You Live

A successful interior is about more than appearance. It needs to work naturally within the architecture of the home and support the way you live.

That might mean a highly organised SieMatic kitchen designed around entertaining, a calm bathroom with a focus on wellness, or a carefully detailed scheme where every finish has been selected to complement the wider interior.

We take time to understand the character of each project and the people using it, creating interiors that feel individual rather than formulaic.

## From Chester Across Cheshire & Beyond

Our Chester showroom welcomes clients from across the region, including Cheshire, the Wirral and North Wales, as well as homeowners and professional project teams further afield.

We have worked on projects across the North West and throughout the UK, building relationships with private clients, architects, interior designers, developers and other property professionals.

For local clients, our Chester location provides the opportunity to experience our products and design approach in person. For projects further afield, our experience and established manufacturer relationships allow us to support schemes beyond Cheshire.
