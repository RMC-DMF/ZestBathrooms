## Frequently Asked Questions

Do I Need an appointment to visit the showroom?

No. We warmly welcome you to browse our luxury Chester showroom for inspiration. If you’d like to discuss your project in more detail, [**private design consultations are available by appointment**](/contact-us), giving you dedicated time with one of our experienced designers to explore ideas, products and tailored design solutions.

How long does a design consultation take?

Most consultations last up to an hour, giving us plenty of time to discuss your ideas, layout, products and budget.

What should I bring?

If possible, bring room measurements, floor plans, architectural drawings or inspiration images. These help us tailor our advice to your project.

Who are your consultants for?

We work with homeowners, architects, interior designers, developers and self-build clients across Chester, Cheshire, Wirral, North Wales, Knutsford, Wilmslow, Tarporley and Nantwich.
