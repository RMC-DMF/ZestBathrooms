OUR STORY

Founded in 1992, Zest began as a specialist bathroom showroom and has evolved into a destination for luxury kitchens, bespoke bathrooms, and considered interior design. For over three decades, our philosophy has remained the same — to create spaces that not only look beautiful but feel effortless to live in. Every project begins with connection: understanding how our clients live and translating that into design that feels personal, balanced, and timeless.

What often begins as a single project grows into a lasting relationship — built on trust, shared vision, and a love of beautiful design. At Zest, we’re proud to be part of our clients’ journeys, creating new spaces and new memories together, time and again.

Today, Zest continues to champion craftsmanship, innovation, and European design excellence, bringing together the world’s leading brands and our own expertise to shape interiors that stand the test of time.
