WHAT WE DO

At Zest, we create bathrooms and kitchens where design and lifestyle come together in harmony. Every project is shaped around your vision, with a focus on timeless elegance, intelligent innovation, and seamless functionality.

Through exclusive partnerships with world-leading brands such as Gessi, AXOR, and Oasis, we deliver spaces defined by refined luxury, meticulous craftsmanship, and enduring quality. From first concept to completion, we ensure every detail is considered, every finish perfected, and every space feels truly exceptional.

## Bespoke Design & Consultation

At Zest, every project begins with you. Our bespoke bathroom and kitchen design service is tailored to your lifestyle, taste, and space. From the first consultation, we take time to understand your vision and translate it into a design journey that is truly personal. With exclusive expertise and a passion for detail, we create spaces that feel effortless, refined, and completely unique to you.

## Luxury Brands & Partnerships

We partner with the world’s leading luxury bathroom and kitchen brands to bring unparalleled quality into your home. From the precision of SieMatic kitchens to the elegance of Bette baths, the innovation of [Gessi brassware](/about/gessi-at-zest-bathrooms-kitchens), and the timeless style of Oasis, Zest curates collections that set new standards in design. Our exclusive access ensures every product we recommend has been carefully chosen for its beauty, performance, and lasting craftsmanship.

## Innovative Wellness & Design

At Zest, we believe your home should be a sanctuary. That’s why we integrate innovative solutions such as Effegibi steam rooms, wellness showers, and Sunshower technology into our designs. More than functionality, our spaces are crafted for indulgence and comfort — where intelligent living meets refined luxury. Every element is selected not only to enhance your home, but to enrich your everyday life.

## Residential & Commercial Expertise

Zest brings luxury design expertise to both private residences and large-scale commercial projects. From elegant home renovations to prestigious developments, we apply the same commitment to excellence and creativity. Our portfolio includes landmark projects that showcase the trust leading developers and discerning homeowners place in our work, reflecting our reputation for quality, innovation, and timeless style.
