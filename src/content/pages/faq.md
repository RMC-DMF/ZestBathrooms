Who are Zest Bathrooms & Kitchens?

Zest is a leading supplier and designer of luxury bathrooms and kitchens across Cheshire, Manchester, north Wales and and beyond. We specialise in bespoke solutions for homes and large-scale projects, curating timeless, high-quality interiors that combine exceptional craftsmanship with innovative design

Do you only supply, or do you also design bathrooms and kitchens?

At Zest, we provide complete kitchen and bathroom solutions, from initial consultation and 3D design concepts to the supply. Installation is carried out by our trusted network of contractors, ensuring every project is completed with precision and excellence.

Which brands do you work with?

For kitchens, we work exclusively with SieMatic — the world-renowned German brand celebrated for its precision engineering, timeless design, and intelligent functionality.

For bathrooms, we partner with world leading brands celebrated for luxury and craftsmanship, offering collections that combine cutting-edge design with effortless sophistication.

Explore our full portfolio [**Brands.**](/brands) 

Can I visit your showroom?

Yes. Our Chester showroom showcases the latest in kitchen and bathroom design, from contemporary SieMatic kitchens to elegant bathroom displays. Visiting allows you to experience the quality, finishes, and smart storage solutions in person.

Do you offer bespoke design?

Absolutely. Every Zest project is tailored to your lifestyle and space. Our designers create custom kitchens and bathrooms that reflect your personal style, whether that’s minimal and modern, classic and timeless, or bold and creative.

Where do you operate?

We serve clients across Cheshire, Manchester, and London, with projects extending nationwide. Our projects range from private homes to high-end  residential developments, hotels, and commercial schemes.

Do you install as well as supply?

For installation, we work alongside a network of trusted contractors to ensure every project is delivered to the highest standard

What makes Zest different from other kitchen and bathroom companies?

At Zest, we blend award-winning design expertise, premium international brands, and sustainable solutions. Our approach is highly collaborative, meaning we listen carefully to your needs and create designs that are beautiful, functional, and future-ready.

Do you work with architects and interior designers?

Yes. We regularly collaborate with architects, developers, and interior designers to deliver premium bathrooms and kitchens for residential and commercial projects.

How do I get started with Zest?

Simply book a design consultation at our showroom or online. We’ll discuss your project, explore your ideas, and create a tailored proposal.

What makes SieMatic kitchens different?

Timeless German kitchen design is built on enduring principles, not fleeting trends. SieMatic kitchens express architectural clarity through refined lines, balanced proportions and materials that mature beautifully. 

Can small kitchens benefit from SieMatic design?

No space is too small or awkward to be exceptional. With minimalist forms, clever spatial planning, and ultra-efficient storage solutions, SieMatic kitchens expand and elevate even the most compact layouts.
