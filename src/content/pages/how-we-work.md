How We Work

At Zest Bathrooms & Kitchens, we work alongside developers, architects, interior designers, consultants and construction professionals to provide specialist [kitchen and bathroom specification](/projects) and supply for residential, hospitality, leisure and commercial projects across the UK.

From our base in Chester, we support projects across Cheshire and the North West, as well as in Manchester, Liverpool, Birmingham, London and locations throughout the UK. We work as part of your wider project team, providing specialist product knowledge, technical expertise and reliable supply.

You lead the project. We support the specification.

Our role is to bring trusted manufacturer relationships, detailed product knowledge and practical supply expertise to your project — helping you select and source the right products while working within your design requirements, budget and programme.

THE PROCESS

## From Specification to Supply

We keep our role focused and straightforward, supporting your team through the stages where our expertise adds the most value.

**Understand the Brief**  
We review your project requirements, design intent, specifications and product needs.

**Product Selection**  
We help identify suitable products, finishes and manufacturers that meet the aesthetic, technical and commercial requirements of the project.

**Specification & Quotation**  
We provide detailed product information, specifications, quantities and project quotations to support procurement and decision-making.

**Coordinate Supply**  
Once products are approved, we coordinate ordering and supply, keeping communication clear throughout the process.

**Project Support**  
We remain available to your team throughout the supply process, providing product knowledge and support when required.

## Supporting Projects Across Multiple Sectors

Our experience spans a broad range of project types, including:

**Residential** – new-build homes, private residences, apartments and multi-unit developments.

**Hospitality** – hotels, guest accommodation and hospitality environments.

**Leisure & Wellness** – leisure facilities, spas, wellness spaces and associated developments.

**Commercial** – commercial interiors, mixed-use developments and other professional environments.

Whether you are working on a single property or a large-scale development, we provide a flexible approach tailored to the requirements of your project.

We understand that professional projects require accuracy, responsiveness and consistency. Our experienced team works around your existing processes, providing the specialist bathroom and kitchen knowledge you need without taking over your project.
