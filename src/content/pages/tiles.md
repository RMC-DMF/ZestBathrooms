## Luxury Bathroom & Kitchen Tiles

The right tile can transform an interior. It can create a sense of space, introduce texture and colour, define different areas or provide a quiet backdrop for statement sanitaryware and furniture.

Our collection includes tiles suitable for walls and floors, with options ranging from understated natural stone effects and elegant marble looks to concrete, wood, metal and decorative finishes.

For bathrooms, tiles can create a cohesive scheme from floor to wall, while in [luxury kitchens](/kitchens/siematic-kitchens) they can complement cabinetry, worktops and architectural details.

Whether you are looking for bathroom tiles in Chester, kitchen tiles in Chester or distinctive surfaces for a wider interior project, our design team can help you select a collection that works with the overall scheme.

## Porcelain & Ceramic Tiles

Porcelain tiles offer an exceptional combination of durability, versatility and design. Available in an extensive range of colours, finishes, formats and effects, they can recreate the character of natural materials while offering the practical benefits required for contemporary interiors.

Our collections include porcelain stoneware, ceramic wall tiles and specialist finishes, with options for bathrooms, kitchens, living spaces and selected outdoor applications.

From refined stone and marble effects to contemporary concrete and architectural surfaces, we can help you find a porcelain tile that complements your design.

## Large-Format Tiles

Large-format tiles can create a clean, architectural look with fewer grout lines and a strong sense of continuity.

They are particularly effective in [contemporary bathrooms](/bathrooms) and open-plan interiors, where larger surfaces can make a space feel calm and considered.  
Our tile suppliers offer a range of large formats and porcelain slabs, allowing you to create everything from understated walls and floors to dramatic statement surfaces.

Large-format tiles require careful planning and installation, so our team can advise on suitable products, formats and applications as part of the design process.
