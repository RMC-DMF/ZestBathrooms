WELLNESS AT ZEST

At the pinnacle of wellness design, [Effe of Italy](#effe-wellness) and [Sunshower of the Netherlands](#sunshower-netherlands) come together to craft sanctuaries that transcend everyday bathing. These visionary brands merge Italian spa artistry with advanced Dutch light therapy, transforming bathrooms into restorative spaces that nourish both body and mind.

From the cleansing purity of steam to the revitalising warmth of infrared light, every element is conceived to promote wellbeing, heighten relaxation, and enrich daily rituals with refined luxury.

INTRODUCING SUNSHOWER

## The Power of Hydrotherapy

Sunshower redefines the shower experience by combining infrared light with water purification for daily rituals that energise and restore. Gentle near-infrared warmth penetrates deep into the body, promoting circulation, skin health, and mood balance — all while you shower.

INTRODUCING EFFEGIBI

## The Art of Heat & Steam

Effe has mastered the tradition of thermal bathing, creating saunas, steam rooms, and hammams that merge wellness science with contemporary design. Every installation is a balance of luxury materials, advanced technology, and therapeutic benefits.
