A long island runs the length of the room and does three jobs: cooking at one end, the sink in the middle, and a raised breakfast bar in thick dark oak along the living-room side, so whoever is sitting there faces the room and not the hob.

The island is clad in dark timber with a thin bronze light line under the top. The tall wall behind is the opposite, a calm grey lacquer that hides the fridge and larder and frames the Gaggenau ovens and coffee machine in a single row.
