Set within a beautifully renovated Georgian house in Tarporley, this bespoke SieMatic SLX kitchen was designed to create a seamless transition between the original property and a striking contemporary glass extension.

Clean architectural lines, integrated LED lighting and a refined material palette bring a modern aesthetic to the space while remaining sympathetic to the character of the home. Ceramic work surfaces provide durability and understated elegance, while an innovative units-on-units configuration maximises the available ceiling height, delivering exceptional storage and visual impact. The result is a light-filled, highly functional kitchen that successfully bridges traditional architecture with contemporary living.
