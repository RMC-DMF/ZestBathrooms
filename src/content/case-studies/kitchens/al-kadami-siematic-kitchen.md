The room is all glass on two sides, so the kitchen sits along the one solid wall and stays quiet: dark timber tall units and wall cabinets, with a soft taupe lacquer on the base units and the island so the light from the windows carries across.

An antiqued mirror splashback under a line of LED light gives the back wall some depth without competing with the view. The island takes the cooking, with a downdraft induction hob so there is no extractor hanging in the sightline, and bar seating faces the room rather than the wall.

Inside, the drawers are fitted out properly: cutlery inserts in the top, deep pan drawers below, everything on full-extension runners.
