For this project, Zest Bathrooms & Kitchens designed and supplied a bespoke SieMatic SLX kitchen that combines contemporary architectural styling with exceptional functionality. Finished in Terra Larix laminate wood-effect cabinetry, the design introduces warmth, texture, and natural character while maintaining the clean lines and precision detailing synonymous with SieMatic.

A striking Dekton work surface was selected to provide both visual impact and long-lasting durability, complementing the kitchen’s refined material palette. Premium appliances from Gaggenau were seamlessly integrated throughout, delivering professional-level performance and enhancing the overall user experience.

The completed kitchen reflects a considered, design-led approach, creating a sophisticated living space that balances timeless aesthetics, innovative functionality, and everyday practicality.
