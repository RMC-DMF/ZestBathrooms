Hansgrohe is the brassware most of our bathrooms are built on. Made in the Black Forest since 1901, it is the range installers trust and the one we reach for when a bathroom has to work every day for twenty years. Showers, basin and bath mixers, and the concealed valves behind them are on display in Chester in a working water area alongside AXOR, so you can feel the spray patterns before you choose.
