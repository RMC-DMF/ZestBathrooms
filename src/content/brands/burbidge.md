Burbidge & Son is one of the oldest kitchen makers in Britain. Henry Burbidge set up as a woodworker in Coventry in 1867, and the company is still family-owned and still makes its kitchens there, with the fifth generation now in charge.

We design with Burbidge when a room calls for a classic kitchen: painted shaker doors, in-frame looks, a dresser or a larder, in a colour chosen for the house. It is the traditional counterpart to SieMatic in our kitchen studio, planned by the same designers through the same free design service.
