We are proud to share that the [SieMatic](https://www.siematic.com/) SG6 has been awarded the Red Dot Design Award 2025. The award is one of the most prestigious design competitions worldwide and only honors products that set standards in terms of design quality and innovation.

At Zest Bathrooms & Kitchens, we partner with world-leading brands like SieMatic to offer our clients timeless design, exceptional craftsmanship, and the latest in kitchen innovation.

## Design That Sets a New Standard

The SG6 impressed the international Red Dot jury with its sophisticated design, high functionality, and striking aesthetic. Recognised as one of the most respected design competitions globally, the Red Dot Design Award honours products that represent true excellence in innovation and design.

As part of SieMatic’s iconic URBAN style, the SG6 brings a fresh, architectural approach to kitchen planning. Its modular and asymmetrical design, combined with open and closed elements, offers new levels of creative freedom.

Elegant rounded shapes and a distinctive handle concept give the SG6 its signature style — bold yet refined, expressive yet timeless.
