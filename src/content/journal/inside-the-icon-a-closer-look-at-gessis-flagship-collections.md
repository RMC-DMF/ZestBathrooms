At Zest, we pride ourselves on curating the finest bathroom brands for our clients and few embody innovation and elegance like [**Gessi**](https://www.gessi.com/en). As a recognised design icon across the world, Gessi continues to redefine what brassware can be: not just functional, but a statement of individuality, artistry, and luxury.

## Design That Speaks for Itself

From the detail and performance of [Hi-Fi](https://www.gessi.com/en/private-wellness/hifi) to the Art Deco-inspired [Venti20](https://www.gessi.com/en/bath/venti20), Gessi’s collections are created with the eye of an artist and the precision of an engineer. Every detail is intentional. Every curve, finish, and mechanism reflects a deep commitment to form and function — underpinned by true Italian craftsmanship.

## Technology Meets Wellbeing

Gessi isn’t just about looks. Their advanced manufacturing techniques, such as PVD coating for ultra-durable finishes and concealed tech integrations, make their products both beautiful and built to last. Collections like [Inciso](https://www.gessi.com/en/bath/inciso), designed in collaboration with David Rockwell, blend tactile materials with contemporary form to enhance both user experience and ambience.

## Why We Work with Gessi

At Zest, we understand the pressure architects, designers and developers face to deliver both design impact and long-term quality. That’s why we consistently recommend Gessi for premium residential, hospitality, and commercial projects across the UK.

Their range offers:

-   Unparalleled finish options — from classic chrome to textured black metal
-   Coordinated product suites for cohesive design
-   Customisable configurations to suit any specification

Whether it’s a penthouse suite, boutique hotel, or statement cloakroom, Gessi offers solutions that transform spaces — and elevate the everyday.
