## Bringing the Benefits of Steam Home with Effe

Steam has been part of wellness rituals for centuries. Traditionally associated with luxury spas and wellness retreats, it is now becoming increasingly desirable within residential bathroom design.

Italian wellness specialist [**Effe**](/about/effe-at-zest-bathrooms-kitchens-2) has spent more than thirty years dedicated exclusively to creating wellness environments and is internationally recognised for its expertise in steam baths, saunas and integrated wellness solutions.

Its steam systems allow designers to incorporate authentic steam experiences directly into contemporary shower spaces, without compromising on aesthetics.

The appeal lies in both the experience and the design flexibility.

Steam creates warmth, encourages relaxation and transforms an everyday shower into a moment of pause within increasingly busy lives. Effe’s systems are designed to integrate discreetly within the architecture of the room, allowing the shower enclosure to remain clean, minimalist and beautifully detailed.

For homeowners, it creates the opportunity to enjoy a luxurious wellness ritual every day.

For interior designers and architects, it provides an elegant specification that combines design, innovation and wellbeing.

For developers, integrated steam experiences can significantly elevate the perception of a premium residential project and help differentiate homes in a competitive market.

## Sunshower: Bringing Light and Warmth into Everyday Living

Our relationship with natural light has changed dramatically.

Modern lifestyles often mean we spend significant amounts of time indoors, particularly during the winter months. This has influenced growing interest in technologies that help create a more uplifting and restorative environment within the home.

[**Sunshower**](/about/sunshower-at-zest-bathrooms-kitchens) takes a unique approach by integrating wellness technology directly within the shower enclosure.

Its slim architectural panels combine carefully controlled low-dose UV light with infrared warmth to create a showering experience that feels both energising and restorative.

The design is intentionally discreet. Rather than appearing as an additional appliance, Sunshower integrates seamlessly into contemporary bathrooms and complements premium materials, minimalist layouts and sophisticated detailing.

For homeowners, it offers an opportunity to transform the daily shower into a moment of comfort and wellbeing.

For designers and architects, it represents another example of how innovative technology can enhance the user experience while maintaining exceptional design integrity.

Increasingly, wellness technologies such as Sunshower are becoming an important consideration within luxury residential projects throughout Chester, Cheshire and the wider North West.
