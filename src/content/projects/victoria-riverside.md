## Victoria Riverside, Manchester

Far East Consortium's Victoria Riverside sits between the green of the River Irk valley and Manchester city centre, in Red Bank. It is 634 apartments, townhouses and maisonettes across three towers and two podium buildings, designed by Hawkins\\Brown, with a residents' lounge, gym, private dining and podium gardens.

Zest supplied all of the Hansgrohe fittings on the scheme, across more than 1,000 bathrooms. Completed August 2026.
