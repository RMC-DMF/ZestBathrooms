The Mount is an exclusive development of four luxury townhouses in Hale Barns, where every detail reflects considered design and premium living.

Partnering with GA Studio, Zest specified and supplied the bespoke bathrooms and SieMatic kitchens for each home, so the two rooms share one palette and one standard of finish. From curated product selection to delivery and site support, the team managed the supply so the architect's vision arrived intact.
